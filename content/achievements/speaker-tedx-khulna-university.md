Delivered a TEDx talk highlighting how women’s contributions in STEM have been historically overlooked and how societal norms often discourage them from pursuing these fields. I discussed the systemic barriers limiting their access to opportunities and proposed actionable solutions fostering a more equitable and inclusive society, starting from ensuring basic rights to creating advanced career pathways.
